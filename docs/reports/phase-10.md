# Phase 10: Conformance and release readiness

Status: complete on the development machine, no open question. The full gate is green locally except the commit message job, which fails on one
body line of an unpushed phase 9 commit (section 5). The pipeline run waits on the branch
being pushed. Built in this
phase:

- the conformance suite a host runs against its own deployment, and the sample host that
  declares only the required values, with in-memory fakes for mail and SMS;
- the contract tests over LIB-API-001: the public surface of every shipped assembly, the
  library-owned schema held to a committed file, and every stored instant held to
  `timestamp with time zone`;
- the release gate: a version is judged at the commit that releases it by what the shipped
  surface and the contract's lists (keys, schema, codes, audit actions, permissions) lost
  or gained, and a commit that breaks the released contract carries the breaking marker;
- the first version's section of `CHANGELOG.md`, prepared under `Unreleased`;
- sign-in, registration and linking with Google and Apple, through the library's own
  client;
- `IResources`, through which a host registers and moves its records;
- a retention floor for each data category, taken from the declaration;
- the Milestone 1 exit sweep: every criterion of chapters 01 to 10, 17 and 20, and of the
  code-side items of 06, 12 and 19, was held against the tests, and every gap the sweep
  found is closed in this phase or listed in section 2.

Defects of earlier phases found by the sweep are fixed, with their tests in section 1
and, where a decision was needed, their entries in section 4:

- `IResources` never registered records (entry 352);
- a host's data category with no retention floor (entry 358);
- a mixed-script username answered as a malformed one;
- the contract-kind tests of three projects, and three unmarked classes, run by no job;
- a denial recorded without the grant that decided it, so its resolution disagreed with the
  live explanation (entry 388);
- a registration link fingerprint compared in variable time;
- throttled answers without `retryAt`, and step-up factors presented with no delay
  (entries 398 to 402);
- nine endpoints calling their service past its contract (entry 408);
- eight operations reading a row before the gate, the group and grant answers telling
  whether a row exists (entries 409 to 412);
- an organization name judged without its comparison key, and missing body members reaching
  the services as empty text (entries 413, 414);
- a first administrator created without the age screen (entry 415).

The decisions taken in the owner's absence are in section 4 and, in the same words, in
`docs/reports/decisions-pending-review.md`.
